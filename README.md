## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1.Farmers register on the platform.
2.Farmers upload crop details, quantity, and price.
3.Consumers or buyers browse available products.
4.Orders are placed directly with farmers.
5.Logistics partners deliver products.
6.Digital payments are made directly to farmers.

## Technical Approach
1. Farmer Registration and Verification

Farmers register through a mobile application using their mobile number and identity details. The system verifies the farmer's information and creates a digital profile containing farm location, crop details, and production capacity.

2. Product Listing

Farmers upload information about available produce, including:

Crop name
Quantity
Quality grade
Harvest date
Selling price

A voice-based feature can be provided to help farmers who are not comfortable typing.

3. AI-Based Price Recommendation

The AI engine analyzes:

Current market prices
Seasonal trends
Local demand and supply
Historical sales data

Based on this analysis, the system suggests an optimal selling price that maximizes farmer profits while remaining competitive for consumers.

4. Product Discovery and Ordering

Consumers, retailers, restaurants, and bulk buyers can browse products using filters such as:

Location
Crop type
Price range
Quality

After selecting products, buyers place orders directly through the application.

5. Smart Logistics Management

Once an order is placed:

The system identifies the nearest delivery partner.
Route optimization algorithms determine the most efficient delivery path.
Real-time tracking is provided to both farmers and buyers.

This reduces transportation costs and delivery time.

6. Secure Digital Payments

Payments are processed through UPI, digital wallets, or banking gateways.

The payment workflow:

Consumer makes payment.
Platform verifies transaction.
Payment is transferred directly to the farmer.
Digital receipts are generated for transparency.
7. Demand Forecasting Engine

The AI module continuously analyzes:

Previous sales data
Seasonal demand patterns
Festival and regional consumption trends

The system provides recommendations about which crops are likely to have higher demand in upcoming seasons, helping farmers plan production effectively.

8. Data Analytics Dashboard

A dashboard provides insights such as:

Daily sales
Revenue earned
Popular products
Customer demand trends
Market price fluctuations
